How a night works
1Your nine batters face the pitcher in order, left to right. Each one either makes an out or reaches base.
2Batters who reach base become runners. Later hits push them forward. A runner who gets all the way around scores a run.
3Three outs ends the inning and wipes the runners. You play three innings — and the order carries over, it does not reset.
4Score the target to beat tonight's pitcher and move up. Miss it and the run ends. Beat all five to win.
